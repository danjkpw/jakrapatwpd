Digital Forensics and Analysis

Cybersecurity Digital Forensics

Overview

This project focuses on digital forensics and analysis in a controlled environment, involving the investigation of system artifacts, logs, and files to identify potential security incidents. The work included examining evidence, analyzing system activity, and reconstructing events to understand what occurred on the target system. Through this project, practical experience was gained in handling digital evidence, identifying suspicious behavior, and applying forensic techniques to support incident investigation and analysis.

Tools

Kali Linux, Volatility, Wireshark, Autopsy and Ghidra
